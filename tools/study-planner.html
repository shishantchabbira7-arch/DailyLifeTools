The Pomodoro Technique

Developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s, the technique utilizes timed sprints to train sustained mental focus and prevent brain fatigue.

1
Choose your subject: Select one specific topic, chapter, or problem set.
2
Eliminate distractions: Silence phone notifications and close unrelated browser tabs.
3
Work with intensity: Commit completely until the audio bell chime rings.
4
Rest and recharge: Stand up, hydrate, stretch, and let your brain integrate the concepts.
