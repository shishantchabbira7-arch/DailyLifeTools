Privacy Policy

Transparency is our core architectural pillar. We designed DailyLife Tools from day one so that we cannot access your personal files even if we wanted to.

1. Zero Server Upload Architecture

All operations performed on DailyLife Tools — including PDF compression, image optimization, password generation, resume compilation, and text transformations — take place strictly within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and local JavaScript APIs. Your files, text snippets, and generated credentials are never uploaded, transmitted, or cached on our servers.

2. Browser Local Storage

Certain tools (such as the Daily Task Planner, Starred Favorites, and Expense Tracker) utilize your browser's native localStorage mechanism to save your settings and preferences between visits. This data resides solely on your physical device. You can erase this data at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Clear All" within the respective tool.

3. Monetag & Third-Party Advertising

To maintain all tools as 100% free without charging subscriptions or placing watermarks, we display non-intrusive advertisements served by trusted ad networks such as Monetag. These ad networks may use standard cookies or web beacons to serve contextually relevant ads. These third-party cookies do not have access to your uploaded files or local utility operations.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not store, harvest, or monetize personal identifiable information (PII) on our servers, users in the European Union (GDPR) and California (CCPA) benefit from built-in privacy by design. We respect Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals.

5. Contacting Us About Privacy

If you have questions about our cryptographic implementation or privacy safeguards, feel free to reach out to our privacy officer at privacy@dailylife.tools.
